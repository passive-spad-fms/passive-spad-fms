01 · The key insight

Don't rebuild the picture.
Understand the photons.

Today's pipelines force SPAD data back into the shape of a conventional image — averaging thousands of binary frames, denoising, then handing the result to a vision model trained on ordinary photos. That step throws away the very thing that makes SPADs special: time.

Native to the sensor

Models consume sparse 1-bit frames and photon timing as-is, learning the Bernoulli statistics of photon arrival instead of fighting them.

Foundation, not one-off

Large-scale pretraining on simulated and real photon data yields general representations that adapt to detection, segmentation, tracking, and depth with little labelled data.

Passive by design

No lasers, no active illumination. Just ambient light — even starlight — so systems stay low-power, eye-safe, and covert.

03 · Vision in extreme conditions

Where conventional cameras give up

Three regimes that break CMOS sensors — and where single-photon sensing, paired with the right models, shines.

CMOSSPAD

Low lightEvery photon counts

CMOS sensors add read noise to every pixel, every frame, burying faint signals. SPADs count individual photons with essentially zero read noise — the limiting factor becomes physics, not electronics.

33 ms exposure160 µs of photons

High speedFreeze motion at 100k fps

Binary frames arrive every few microseconds. Models choose their own temporal window — short for fast motion, long for static scenes — eliminating motion blur by construction.

SPAD Conventional

High dynamic rangeNo hard clipping

Conventional pixels saturate abruptly at full-well capacity. A SPAD's response rolls off smoothly, so headlights, sun, and shadow stay measurable in the same frame — and a model can read them all.

04 · Applications

Perception for the hardest environments

SPAD arrays are rapidly becoming cheaper and higher-resolution, thanks to their adoption in phones and automotive LiDAR. The missing piece is the software to see with them.

Autonomous vehicles

Pedestrian and obstacle detection on unlit roads, through oncoming headlights and out of tunnel glare — with microsecond reaction windows.

Drones & robotics

Agile flight and manipulation that needs high-rate visual feedback, at dawn, dusk, indoors, or underground.

Defense & security

Passive night surveillance with no emitted light to give away position, and tracking of fast-moving objects.

Space & astronomy

Space-domain awareness, star tracking, and on-orbit inspection where photons are scarce and contrast is extreme.

Industrial inspection

Inspecting high-speed production lines, rotating machinery, and welding or laser processes with extreme brightness.

Life sciences & medical

Low-dose, low-phototoxicity imaging of living samples, and fast fluorescence dynamics.

05 · How we build it

A full stack for photon-native vision

  1. 01

    Physics-accurate photon simulation

    We convert large video corpora into realistic SPAD streams, modelling photon arrival, dark counts, crosstalk, dead time, and sensor-specific quirks.

  2. 02

    Large-scale pretraining

    Self-supervised objectives over sparse binary space-time volumes teach the model to aggregate evidence adaptively across photons, pixels, and time.

  3. 03

    Real-sensor adaptation

    Fine-tuning on captured data from commercial and research SPAD arrays closes the simulation-to-reality gap.

  4. 04

    Task heads & edge deployment

    Lightweight heads for detection, segmentation, depth, optical flow, and tracking, optimized to run alongside the sensor.
